Downloads no longer apply setuid, setgid or sticky bits sent by the server, check that a server-chosen local name matches the requested remote basename (as OpenSSH does since CVE-2019-6111), and refuse to follow an existing local symlink at a server-named path instead of writing through it. The local path passed by the caller may still be a symlink. O_NOFOLLOW suggested in net-sftp#162 by srosenhamer [#104]
