Add a `quote:` option to `upload`/`download` for the remote path: `:posix` (default), `:windows` for Win32-OpenSSH under cmd.exe or PowerShell (double-quoted, `\` sent as `/`, paths containing `"`, `$`, `` ` ``, `%`, `!`, curly quotes, CR, LF or NUL rejected), `:none` to send the path unchanged to servers without a shell, or a callable. By default a leading `~` or `~/` is now left for the remote shell to expand to the home directory; such paths previously referred to a literal `~` entry in the login directory and usually failed.
